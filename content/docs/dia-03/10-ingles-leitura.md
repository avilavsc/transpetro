## Como usar

1. Ler o texto **sem dicionário** (1ª vez)  
2. Marcar conectivos e referentes  
3. Fazer as questões  
4. Anotar só as palavras que atrapalharam

| Tipo | Pista | O que fazer |
|---|---|---|
| Ideia principal | *mainly about*, *best title* | Olhe início/fim e o fio comum |

## Texto de prática

**The Nursing Process**

The nursing process is a methodological tool that guides professional nursing care and its documentation. It is usually organized into five interrelated steps: data collection (or assessment), nursing diagnosis, planning, implementation, and evaluation. According to professional rules, nursing diagnosis and the prescription of nursing interventions are activities reserved for the nurse. Technical and auxiliary nursing staff may participate in implementation under the nurse’s supervision. Although the process is often associated with hospitals, it should also be used in outpatient services, workplaces, schools, and home care. Therefore, when applied outside hospitals, it is commonly called a nursing consultation. Continuous evaluation helps the team review results and adapt the care plan.

a) Hospital architecture and equipment

a) Only hygiene care  
b) Nursing diagnosis and prescription of nursing interventions

**4.** The word **Although** in the sentence “Although the process is often associated with hospitals…” indicates:  
a) addition  
b) contrast  
c) place  
d) obligation  

**5.** In the sentence “it is commonly called a nursing consultation”, **it** refers to:  
a) the hospital only  
b) the nursing process when applied outside hospitals  
c) the auxiliary nurse  
d) the laboratory result  

**6.** It can be inferred that technical nursing staff:  
a) replace the nurse in diagnosis  
b) may help in implementation under supervision  
c) never participate in the nursing process  
d) are responsible for the whole care plan alone
