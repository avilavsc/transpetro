**Pistas de questão**
- *mainly about / best title* → ideia principal  
- *according to the text* → detalhe no texto  
- *implies / can be inferred* → conclusão lógica, sem inventar  
- *refers to* → volte 1–2 frases  
- *closest in meaning* → contexto da frase

## Texto de prática

**Occupational Medical Control Programs**

Occupational medical control programs are designed to protect workers’ health in relation to workplace risks. They usually include clinical evaluations before employment, at regular intervals, and when workers return after long absences. Employers are generally responsible for organizing and paying for these evaluations. Although nurses play an important role in education, immunization, and worker support, the technical responsibility for the medical program is typically assigned to an occupational physician. According to prevention principles, these programs should not be used as a tool for unfair selection of candidates. Early detection of work-related health problems can reduce absenteeism and improve safety at work.
